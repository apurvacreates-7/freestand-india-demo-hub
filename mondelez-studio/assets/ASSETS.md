These files identify platforms and brands throughout Sources, Exports, Campaigns, Cohorts, People and the workspace shell. Freestand’s sidebar uses its actual wordmark with the white CSS treatment used by the official sampling app. Generic data transports use Lucide icons.

Logos are bundled locally; there is no dependency on remote favicon or image services during a presentation. Their presence does not imply a vendor partnership, connector certification or production integration.
